Henderson Project - January Strategy Meeting
Date: January 15, 2025
Attendees: Sarah Chen (PM), David Okafor (Eng Lead), Lisa Martinez (VP Product)

Summary:
The Henderson project was discussed at length. Three options are currently on the table:

- Option A: Full rebuild of the existing infrastructure. Estimated 6 months, $2.1M budget.
- Option B: Incremental migration with a phased rollout. Estimated 4 months, $1.4M budget.
- Option C: Partnership with an external vendor (Stratton Systems). Estimated 3 months, $1.8M budget.

Sarah noted that stakeholder feedback has been mixed. The engineering team has strong preferences but the board wants to see a cost-benefit analysis before committing.

Action items:
- David to prepare technical feasibility report for all three options by Feb 1
- Lisa to schedule board presentation for late February
- Sarah to gather additional stakeholder input

No decision was reached at this meeting. The team agreed to revisit after the feasibility report is complete.
