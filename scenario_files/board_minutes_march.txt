CONFIDENTIAL - BOARD DECISION

Henderson Project - Final Decision
Board Meeting: March 3, 2025

Decision: OPTION B APPROVED

The board unanimously approved Option B (incremental migration with phased rollout).

Key terms:
- Budget: $1.35M (under the $1.5M ceiling)
- Timeline: 4 months, starting April 1
- Phase 1 (core migration): April-May
- Phase 2 (feature parity): June-July
- David Okafor to lead implementation
- Monthly progress reviews with the board

Rationale: Option B was selected for its lower risk profile, ability to execute with the current team, and budget efficiency. The board acknowledged Option A's technical merits but deemed the hiring requirements and timeline risk unacceptable given Q2 budget pressures.

Signed: Lisa Martinez (VP Product), James Wright (CFO)
